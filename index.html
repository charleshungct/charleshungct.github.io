About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Imperial College London, entering my penultimate year, working where mechanical design, controls and robotics meet — mostly underwater. I co-founded ICAUV, Imperial's autonomous underwater vehicle society, where I lead the design of a modular swarm AUV for seabed mapping, and I spent summer 2026 building my own AUV from the hull up: fabrication, electronics, control and software. Before that I was Chief Mechanical Engineer of Fish Logic, which placed 2nd at the MATE ROV World Championship 2023 and 1st in Teleprescence 2022.

Away from the water I've built an ion thruster, designed a liquid rocket engine injector and Formula Student components, and done a mechanical and controls internship at Zelostech. What interests me most is the gap between a pool test and a deployed system — the controls, perception and mechanical reliability that close it.

Projects

Modular swarm AUV — ICAUV

Underwater roboticsMechanical design

A modular, inline AUV for seabed mapping, designed so modules can be added or swapped without touching the pressure housing. A continuous four-rod external spine carries all loads; printed modules clamp onto it with split clamps, and the acrylic canister sits in rubber-lined saddles with radial O-ring endcaps — it's never a structural member. An electronics sled on one endcap gives routine access without pulling the canister. Target depth 10–50 m.
